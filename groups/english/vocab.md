# Vocabulary

## 2026-03-01

• *ontology* — a structured framework that defines relationships and rules in a system
  _Context:_ "treat ontology as your core contract"

• *moat* — a competitive advantage that protects a company from competitors (business term)
  _Context:_ "The real moat isn't the model, it's the operational graph it's allowed to touch"

• *bake in* — to build something into a system from the start, not add it later
  _Context:_ "Bake security, observability, and evals in horizontally, not as afterthoughts"

• *afterthought* — something added later without proper planning
  _Context:_ "Bake security, observability, and evals in horizontally, not as afterthoughts"

• *blueprint* — a detailed plan or model that others can follow
  _Context:_ "Palantir's End-to-End Agentic Architecture is a useful blueprint for the rest of us"

• *connective tissue* — the infrastructure or systems that link different parts together (metaphor)
  _Context:_ "how much of the real value comes from the connective tissue, not the model itself"

• *compound* — to grow or accumulate over time (in business: value builds on itself)
  _Context:_ "That is where enterprise value will compound"
